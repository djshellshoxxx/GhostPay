Chaining

You can stack options from different stages: delay the payment, scan through your own node, sweep to a fresh destination, then optionally exit through a pool. Each step covers a different leak. Each one also costs time or fees, and a mistake in any step can undo the rest. Options at the same stage often conflict or overlap, which is why the full list is a menu, not a switch.

Network layers

Network tools such as Tor, I2P, Nym and Oblivious HTTP hide who you are from the servers you talk to (host, RPC, relayer). They do nothing about what is written on-chain. In GhostPay they are planned profiles, not a built feature.

The full cards, with what each option does not hide, are on the Privacy options page.
