A sweep moves funds from a one-time address to your own wallet. A one-time address starts with no gas coin, so a relayer can pay gas and take a fee from the tokens.

{{diagram:sweep}}

In plain words

  • Signed order. Your single signature fixes the destination, amount and fee. The relayer can send it or not; it cannot edit it.
  • Nonce. A one-use number inside the order. The contract marks it used before moving anything, so the same order cannot run twice.
  • Fee split. Net amount to your destination, fee to the fee wallet you signed. The fee is the larger of gas cost times 1.10 and 0.50 %, capped at 3 %.
  • Direct sweep is the alternative: put gas coin on the one-time address and send an ordinary transfer yourself.

The sweep is public. Anyone can link the one-time address to your destination, so use a fresh destination. In the app the key is used inside the page and there is no wallet pop-up, so read the destination on screen before signing.
