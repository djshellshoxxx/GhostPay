A policy account is a small contract that can only pay one fixed destination, after a delay, within per-token limits. The goal: a stolen key cannot quietly send funds elsewhere.

{{S:experimental}} Contracts and a client library only. No app support, no recovery path, not deployed, unaudited. Do not use.

{{diagram:policy}}

Trade-offs

  • The destination is fixed in advance, so the link from your payments to that destination is permanent. This is the opposite of the fresh-destination advice for normal sweeps.
  • A delay protects you and also slows you. Changing the destination takes twice the delay.
  • The policy hash is public in your meta-address; the destination itself is not revealed by it.
  • Creation has a flat fee (design value, owner to confirm). The usual 0.25 % checkout fee also applies when paying through the policy router.
