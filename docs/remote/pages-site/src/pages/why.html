A public blockchain is a public ledger. If a shop gives every customer the same address, anyone can read its sales, balance and customers. GhostPay tries to fix one piece of that: address reuse.

Read this first. GhostPay is unaudited, testnet-only and not deployed anywhere. It reduces address reuse. It does not make payments anonymous or untraceable.

The idea in three lines

  1. The seller shares one public meta-address.
  2. Every payment goes to a fresh one-time address that only the seller can find and spend from.
  3. Invoices travel in the link itself, after the #, so the web server never receives them.

What we stand for

Runs in your browser

Keys are made and used on your device. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no third-party scripts.

Limits stated up front

The payer, amount, token, time and the use of the router stay public. We say so on the first page of the app.

No back door in the contracts

The contracts are written with no owner, proxy, pause or admin function. They are not deployed and not independently audited, so treat this as a design goal to be checked.

Fees you can read

0.25 % when you pay through the router. Paying the one-time address directly costs no GhostPay fee. See the fee model.

What we do not claim

  • Not anonymous. A payer can follow funds from the one-time address to the wallet you sweep to.
  • Not audited by a third party. Only self-run and AI-assisted reviews exist.
  • Not live. No contract is deployed and the app disables payment without a deployment manifest.
  • Not for real money. Ethereum Sepolia and Base Sepolia testnets only.
  • Not finished. Most privacy options on the options page are planned, not built.

Manifesto, short

Reduce what a stranger can learn from your address. Say plainly what is still visible. Never ship a claim the code cannot back. Put the keys in the user’s hands, and the warnings on the first screen.
