One public address in, a fresh one-time address per payment, and a sweep to your own wallet. Here is the whole path.

{{diagram:flow}}

In three acts

1. The seller sets up

Creates keys in the browser, backs them up, shares the meta-address and sends an invoice link.

2. The payer pays

Opens the link, reviews every amount, pays from their own wallet. A public announcement is posted.

3. The seller collects

Scans announcements with the viewing key and sweeps funds to a destination wallet.

What this does not hide

The payer address, amount, token, time and the use of the GhostPay router are public on-chain. Sweeping links the one-time address to your destination wallet, so use a fresh destination. See what an observer sees.
