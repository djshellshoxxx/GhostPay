# Phase B review: F6 payment delay (jitter)

Date: 2026-10-21. Reviewer: documentation author (self-run; not independent). Roadmap: Feature 6. Related audits: 01 (F6-1..F6-4), 03 (S3-13 notes jitter as safe).

## Scope reviewed

`apps/web/src/lib/jitter.ts` (sampling and scheduler), the delay section of `apps/web/src/views/Pay.tsx`, Settings (minimum/maximum delay).

## What the feature does

An opt-in delay drawn from a user-visible range (default 120-900 seconds in `settings.ts`) before the payer may send. The sample uses the browser CSPRNG with rejection sampling. Nothing is signed or queued while waiting; the payer can cancel or send immediately; when the timer ends the app revalidates the invoice expiry, quote and manifest state and then shows the send button. It never submits automatically. The timer uses a monotonic clock; the tab must stay open.

## Evidence (existing tests)

| Check | Test |
|---|---|
| Sampling is in range, unbiased | `jitter.test.ts`: "maps samples into range", "rejects biased samples (rejection sampling) and retries", "is uniform within tolerance over many draws (real CSPRNG)", "validates the range", "gives up if the source is stuck above the limit" |
| Monotonic timer, wall-clock jump | "waits, then becomes ready, using the monotonic clock", "is not shortened by a wall-clock jump forward within the invoice validity" |
| Expiry during the wait | "expires when the invoice expires before the delay ends"; `Pay.test.tsx` "delay: revalidates at the due time and stops if the invoice expired while waiting" |
| Cancel is final; listeners cleaned | "can be cancelled, which is final"; "unsubscribes listeners and uses real timers by default" |
| Nothing signed while waiting; cancel; skip | `Pay.test.tsx`: "delay: nothing is signed while waiting, can be cancelled, or skipped with \"send now\""; "delay: becomes ready by itself after the (zero) interval" |
| Honest wording | audit 01 F6-3 (grep: no "Securing Transmission"); in-app text: "timing friction, not anonymity" |

## Checklist (Roadmap F6)

- [x] CSPRNG with rejection sampling, bounds visible.
- [x] No auto-submit; explicit final confirmation; cancel and "send now".
- [x] Revalidation at due time (expiry, quote, chain, fees).
- [x] Language describes timing friction only.
- [ ] Nonce re-read at send time: left to the wallet; not separately tested.
- [ ] Sleep / suspended tab / system-clock changes tested in a **browser**: only unit tests with a fake clock exist (audit F6-4). A suspended mobile tab may fire late or never.
- [ ] Measured whether it provides any value: no measurement exists.

## Residual risks and gaps

1. **It does not provide anonymity.** The final transaction is public and linked to the payer; the delay only makes crude timing correlation between the payer opening a link and the payment less exact. No cover traffic exists. Users may over-trust it: the copy and the [privacy limits](../user/privacy-limits.md) page say it is friction only.
2. **A longer window raises the chance of a changed quote or gas price;** revalidation handles it by stopping, so the user restarts.
3. **Closing the tab cancels,** by design (nothing persists).
4. **Dependent on timers in background tabs,** which browsers throttle; behaviour on mobile Safari is untested.

## Verdict

Low risk. Keep the wording modest; add a browser-level test for backgrounded tabs before relying on exact bounds.
