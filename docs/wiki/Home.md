## Security and privacy boundaries

Local browser key handling does not hide on-chain amounts, transfer histories, timing or wallet relationships. A seller-signed receipt alone does not establish payment settlement. Downloading an encrypted backup does **not** establish recoverability. The owner's unmerged #157 integration includes the GP-PAGES-02 verification and GP-PAGES-04 sleep-aware lock changes, has corrected the historical #150 Settings test assertion, and passed its exact-head application CI; owner approval, external-wallet acceptance and independent review remain outstanding.

Without an approved, signed EVM **testnet** manifest, the Pages artifact is a **payments-disabled demonstration**. Promotion also requires SonarCloud Security Rating A, ADR-0029 approval, a dedicated hosting-origin decision, owner-approved signing keys/manifests, external-wallet/testnet acceptance, independent security/provenance review and explicit merge, deployment, Wiki publication and release authorization. Passing CI does not authorize mainnet or real funds.
