## 2. Verifiable behavior on main versus unmerged sources

### Invoice and payment expiry

[Expiry helper, blob `51b0f6238f0ddd27a6b00a50b705b2372cff1548`](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/packages/invoice-schema/src/expiry.ts) treats `now >= invoice.expiresAt` as expired and validates the injected clock. [`reviewInvoice`](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/apps/web/src/lib/pay.ts) blocks an expired invoice and an expired fiat quote. [`preparePayment`/`sendPayment`](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/apps/web/src/lib/payExec.ts) recheck expiry around asynchronous authorization and before wallet handoff; a user-visible approval can outlive a refused payment. [`GhostPayRouter.sol`](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/packages/contracts/src/GhostPayRouter.sol) enforces a per-call `deadline` with `block.timestamp > deadline`, but **does not persist invoice identity or an invoice-expired state**. The client uses its clock and bounded transaction deadline; those are not independent chain evidence of invoice state. Do not infer that a receipt for an expired invoice is impossible or automatically refundable.

### Ordinary payment funds and refunds

[Router contract](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/packages/contracts/src/GhostPayRouter.sol) forwards net payments immediately to the stealth destination, sends any fee separately, and has **no escrow, ownership, rewind or refund method**. It explicitly warns that forced ETH/direct token transfers into the router are unrecoverable by design. The [merchant billing refund planner, blob `817f0dcea0566f81335d3d76a5a10b160fe6ba77`](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/packages/merchant-billing/src/refunds.ts) constructs idempotent *plans* and transitions `planned → submitted → confirmed/failed`; an `OriginalPayment` is provided by its caller, not verified by this module. [Merchant refund CLI, blob `1780d3893f898522b794a77d394eae6029b197de`](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/packages/cli/src/commands/merchant-refund.ts) reports `authorityVerified:false`, `paymentVerified:false`, `executed:false` and **always refuses `merchant refunds execute`**. The [offline fixture](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/examples/merchant-operations/REFUND_BATCH.md) intentionally never signs, broadcasts or verifies an original real payment. Accordingly: refund planning is *not* real refund authorization, and expiration of a noncustodial payment request does *not* reverse a completed transfer. An authorized merchant may voluntarily send a new transaction using surviving spending authority; **automatic refund is not applicable to the existing direct-transfer architecture**. Real refund authority/execution is missing and requires a separately accepted design/interface; do not promote the fixture.

### Unknown transaction outcome and duplicate sending

[`sendViaWallet`](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/apps/web/src/lib/wallet.ts) writes an unknown attempt before `eth_sendTransaction`, preserves a wallet-returned hash in `WalletBroadcastError`, and treats provider errors without a hash as potentially broadcast. [Attempt journal, blob `b8c2ec1915b833a5b78286b9b9caca1c247e827c`](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/apps/web/src/lib/duplicateAttemptJournal.ts) is **sessionStorage-tab-scoped**, never auto-evicts unresolved attempts, and requires explicit confirmation to clear. [WalletAttempts UI](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/apps/web/src/components/WalletAttempts.tsx) displays these records; it does not independently prove whether a hashless wallet submission was mined.

Two concrete limits follow from source: distinct tabs do not share the attempt store/lock, and a tab's persisted record does not survive site-data deletion. Multi-tab same-invoice blocking therefore is **not established**. Main's `finishAttempt` removes a prior record with the same attempt ID before writing a new completion even if the hash changes; #427 has a test-first fix preserving the first hash and later stacked edge-case follow-ups. That helper defect is confirmed at source level and reported by #427; actual browser fund loss has not been demonstrated. **Do not independently reimplement or merge around #427–#443.**

### Independent keys, sweeps, and lost-owner limits

The main branch lacks `apps/web/src/lib/paymentKeyExport.ts`. [#372 version, blob `443e62404c232922a3a5f485275dcc86cfd1c77f`](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/055d33e042c63edf1d5bbbdd7833d3c1ef93733d/apps/web/src/lib/paymentKeyExport.ts) implements encrypted single-payment and archive export, explicit raw-key export, master backup, independent decoder interoperability, credential reauthorization and recovery controls for supported scheme-1 EVM EOA payments. Its account-code check refuses contract/delegated accounts, and its source reports held-secret wiping and session/expiry revocation. This is **implemented on a draft branch; not verified complete on current main**. Independent key restoration cannot reconstruct keys that were never retained and cannot undo finalized chain transfers.

[Main Sweep journal](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/apps/web/src/lib/sweepAttemptJournal.ts) records unknown/submitted/included/reverted/expired/refused/replaced states in **tab-scoped sessionStorage**, writes prior to network handoff and fails closed for corrupt storage. [Sweep UI](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/apps/web/src/views/Sweep.tsx) preserves uncertain relayed outcomes rather than releasing locks on timeouts and permits controlled same-nonce replacement of an unknown direct sweep. [#372](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/pull/372) includes stricter saved-Sweep JSON, separate balance/signing authority checks, and further recovery UI. These protect one tab, but by themselves do not prove crash recovery after storage deletion, multi-tab locking, or real-chain finality.

[`PolicyAccount.sol`](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/packages/contracts/src/PolicyAccount.sol) restricts payouts to a committed destination but does not rotate a completely lost owner key. [ADR-0028](https://github.com/djshellshoxxx/GhostPay/blob/eb59d93cffed7848c4aac37d0e9650b87239c6da/docs/adr/ADR-0028-policy-recovery-authority-model.md) is explicitly an **unaccepted design model**, not deployable on-chain owner recovery. An accepted recovery authority is an owner/contract-design decision. Never confuse backup restore with loss-of-all-authority recovery.

## 5. Reproduction and qualification plan (not executed in this session)

1. **Expiry:** use a fixed clock at `expiresAt - 1`, `expiresAt`, `expiresAt + 1`; expire during approval, delayed wallet callback and prior successful broadcast; shift/rollback clock; mine before/after calldata deadline; compare stored invoice and receipt lifecycle.
2. **Unknown payment:** mock `eth_sendTransaction` returning a hash after storage write failure, throwing before/after network submission, returning malformed hash, or completing twice with conflicting hashes. Verify first-hash retention, no automatic second send, and reconciliation after reload.
3. **Concurrent tabs:** two isolated `sessionStorage` instances sharing the same wallet/account/invoice; attempt simultaneous send/sweep and crash one after handoff. Show exactly what coordination exists. A source-level storage separation is observed; actual multi-context browser outcome is untested.
4. **Receipts/reorg:** forged/stale/throwing lookup, initial success then removed/reverted receipt, wrong recipient/token/amount, and eventual re-inclusion; never elevate inconclusive evidence to settled.
5. **Sweep:** interrupt before lock, after lock, before/after relay response, with insufficient gas, wrong network, rejected token transfer, stale quote and same-nonce replacement; verify no duplicate nonce and retained recovery authorization.
6. **Backup:** encrypted export → independent decoder → offline signature with cleared originating session; corrupt/tamper bytes, wrong passphrase, wrong viewing key, wrong chain, old format, failed replacement, and termination at each write boundary; ensure no last-copy destruction.
7. **Refund:** with no real authority adapter, assert CLI refuses execute and no wallet/RPC request is made. Test planner fixture replay, partial confirmation and unknown status reservations separately; never represent fixture confirmation as chain confirmation. A future real escrow pathway must get distinct authority/finality/duplicate-submission tests.
