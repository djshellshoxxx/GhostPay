1. Set up and back up

In Seller keys, generate a key set or import your existing encrypted keystore. A new key set initially lives in the current tab. The spending key controls funds, the viewing key discovers payments, and the signing key signs invoices and seller receipts.

  1. Download the encrypted keystore before depending on the keys. The export form requires at least 14 characters and rejects weak passphrases; it also offers a generated suggestion.
  2. Keep the keystore and passphrase in separate protected places. Confirm that the file imports in a separate browser profile before clearing your original session.
  3. If you use the encrypted browser vault, retain a separate file backup. Clearing site data removes local storage; a browser vault is not an external backup.
  4. Back up optional local tags and reconciliation data separately. Their files do not replace the seller keystore. The full build offers a guided key/tag checklist; the lean build keeps the narrower keystore flow.

In Settings, choose the RPC providers used for chain requests. A second independent provider can expose disagreement, but sends queries to another operator. Choose providers whose availability and data practices fit your needs.

2. Create an invoice

Use Create invoice to choose the supported chain, asset, fixed token amount, expiry and fee policy. Review the gross payment, fee and net amount. Optional memo and line items belong to the invoice; encryption protects them only according to the link's selected mode.

A link that includes its decryption key is a bearer link: anyone holding it can read its contents. A passphrase-protected link needs the separate passphrase. Preserve the complete original link or file; a screenshot of selected fields does not preserve its signed data.

Optional registration for local matching starts off. It lets the seller later review a proposed association between an invoice and eligible scan evidence; it does not automatically mark an invoice paid.

3. Review and pay

  1. Open or paste the original invoice in Pay. Check its signature, seller signing key, network, asset, exact amounts and expiry.
  2. Inspect the deployment and transaction review. A valid invoice signature alone does not establish the seller's identity; compare a previously trusted signing key when available.
  3. Connect the intended wallet account on the intended chain. For a token payment, an approval or permit authorizes token use; it is separate from the payment itself.
  4. Read each wallet request and authorize only the reviewed action. Changing the wallet, chain or invoice can invalidate the previous review.
  5. Keep any returned transaction reference and the original local receipt. Check the displayed verification and confirmation state before deciding what happened.

An unknown broadcast outcome may already have been sent. A timeout, disconnected provider or closed wallet prompt is not evidence that nothing happened. Check the wallet and transaction references before clearing an attempt or starting another payment. Clearing a local attempt cannot cancel a transaction.

4. Understand payment evidence

These checks answer different questions
ResultMeaning
Valid invoiceThe invoice passes its format and signature checks. It is a payment request, not evidence of settlement.
Valid seller receipt signatureThe signing key signed those receipt fields. Binding it to an invoice checks their consistency. It does not independently establish payment or a person's identity.
Observed payment or announcementA provider, scan or saved record reports an event. Discovery and imported history must not be treated as verified settlement.
Verified payment evidenceThe supported flow checked the relevant chain, transaction, receipt, recipient, asset and amount. Read its finality and provider limitations; a later reorganization can invalidate earlier evidence.

A block explorer is a useful external view of a transaction. Its operator receives your lookup, its indexing may lag, and providers can disagree. Its display is not a substitute for the app's supported checks or an independent chain verification.

5. Receipts and QR codes

An eligible result in Scan and receive can offer a seller receipt. Load the original signed invoice when binding that receipt, review the matching fields and give the required consent. The lean build requires that validated invoice binding. In the full build, a manually typed digest remains an annotation rather than proof that an invoice was validated.

Download and preserve the original digital receipt file. A QR code is a convenient representation of serialized data. A QR image may be incomplete, degraded or separated from its invoice and verification context; it is not a better long-term record than the original file. Reimport the original receipt in the invoice's receipt-verification controls when you need to check it again.

A payer's local transaction record and a seller-signed receipt are different records. Neither should silently turn an observed or unknown payment into a verified one. Keep the invoice, receipt and relevant transaction references together in a place you control.

6. Choose the correct file

What each import expects
ImportExpected file and purpose
Seller keysThe encrypted GhostPay keystore and its passphrase. It restores secret keys; treat the file as sensitive even though it is encrypted.
Public seller configurationThe public configuration exported by your other device. It previews public-key consistency and supported chains. It cannot restore secret keys.
Invoice for receipt creationThe original signed invoice link or saved text file, plus its passphrase when required. It binds receipt fields to that invoice. The pasted URL is parsed locally, not fetched.
Receipt verificationThe original signed receipt JSON. It checks the signature and expected invoice/payment fields; it is not an encrypted key backup.
Reconciliation restoreAn encrypted reconciliation backup for the same seller viewing key. Review the merge preview before applying; imported observations require fresh verification.
Tag archive restoreThe separate tag archive from the full build and its required unlock information. Read its preview and scope; it does not restore the seller keys.

An incorrect, oversized or malformed file is refused. Choose the intended file and retry, including selecting the same file after correcting a passphrase or other input. Cancelling a review or switching key/network context can invalidate pending work.

7. Exchange public configuration

Seller public configuration can be exported as QR, text or a file. Compare the public keys on both devices before relying on the preview. A checksum detects accidental changes; it does not authenticate who sent the data or prove freshness.

Settings exchange carries only the saved delay range, idle-lock timeout, theme and address-bar-clearing preference. RPC/relayer URLs and pinned signer trust remain local. Apply imported preferences only after reviewing them.

Camera use begins when you request it. If permission is denied or the camera is unavailable, use the supported paste/file alternative. A public registry scan only previews public data; publishing a registry entry is a separate wallet action.

8. Merchant workflow

The web workflow is seller setup, invoice creation, scanning, explicit local reconciliation and receipt handling. Confirm the chain evidence and amount before treating a proposed invoice match as accepted. Restoring a saved history does not independently reverify it.

Subscription schedules, installments, webhooks and shop integrations have separate developer/operator modules. They are not a promise of automatic customer debits or a complete merchant dashboard in this interface. Fixture refund records do not execute a refund. Production merchant authority and integrations require their own acceptance evidence.

9. Troubleshooting

Choose the next step from the actual state
What you seeWhat to do
Payment disabledRead the deployment/network explanation. Selecting a listed testnet does not supply a verified deployment. Do not remove the gate to continue.
Provider failure or disagreementCheck the configured endpoint and chain. A provider response or explorer page may be stale. Refresh evidence before approving an action.
Unknown or pending broadcastInspect the wallet and known transaction references. An unknown result may already be on-chain; a second submission can duplicate the action.
Storage unavailableCheck this browser's site-storage permissions. Retry a transient failure before clearing site data, which deletes saved local records. Export backups first when available.
Backup will not unlockUse the matching file, passphrase and seller key scope. Public configuration and receipt files cannot restore secret keys.
Some text remains EnglishSecurity-sensitive translations keep their English source until reviewed. Changing language does not change signed invoice or receipt bytes.

The full and lean builds intentionally expose different feature sets. Experimental policy-account recovery is not an available lost-key recovery service. GhostPay does not offer calendar/timestamp-server selection or notarization certificates.
