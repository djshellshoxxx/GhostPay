Privacy options

GhostPay offers a menu of privacy options. Most are not built yet. This page lists each one with its real status and what it still leaves visible.

Hidden is not anonymous. Even the best option here leaves the payer, amount, token, time and router call public unless a card says otherwise. Nothing is audited. Testnet only.

How to read the labels

  • BUILT Works in the app (or the running app code) today, in tests and mocks. Not tested against a real wallet, RPC or chain.
  • PLANNED A written spec only. No code. It may change or never ship.
  • EXPERIMENTAL Code exists as a library or contracts, but a user cannot use it from the app, or it is unverified.

Chaining methods

Options are meant to be picked one at a time and stacked: pay with a delay, scan through your own node, sweep to a fresh destination, optionally exit through a pool. Read the map above from top to bottom. Each stacked step adds cost, delay and a new way to make a mistake, and the methods do not add up to anonymity. Only the built options can be used today.

Built

Usable in the app today.

One-time address per payment BUILT

Each payment goes to a new address derived from your meta-address, so an invoice does not reveal which on-chain address is yours.

Does not hide: The payer, amount, token, time and router use. Two invoices from one seller share a meta-address.

Source: README, docs/user/privacy-limits.md

Fragment-only invoice link BUILT

Invoice data sits after the # in the link, which browsers do not send to the web server. You can encrypt line items and memo inside the link.

Does not hide: Anyone holding the link reads the invoice (it is a bearer link). Browsers may keep it in history.

Source: apps/web, docs/user

View-tag scanning BUILT

A 1-byte tag in each announcement lets your scanner skip most non-matching payments quickly.

Does not hide: Nothing. It saves work only; the public log is unchanged.

Source: docs/user/privacy-limits.md item 6; MH-TAG-1

Random payment delay BUILT

Optional wait of random length (you pick the range) before the payer can send. Cancel or send now at any time.

Does not hide: Not anonymity. It is timing friction only. No cover transactions. The tab must stay open.

Source: apps/web Pay + Settings

Gas buffer (opt-in trade-off) BUILT

Adds a little native coin to the one-time address so the seller can pay gas to move tokens.

Does not hide: It is public and links payer to one-time address more visibly. It costs privacy; listed so you can decide.

Source: apps/web Pay

Your own RPC URL BUILT

Choose the RPC endpoint per chain in Settings instead of relying on a default.

Does not hide: The RPC you choose still sees your IP, the addresses you query and what you broadcast.

Source: apps/web Settings; MH-NET-1 (partial)

Direct or relayer sweep BUILT

Sweep yourself (the one-time address needs gas coin) or have a relayer submit a signed order and take a fee from the amount.

Does not hide: The chain still shows one-time address to destination. A relayer additionally sees destination, amount, token, hashed IP and timing.

Source: apps/web Sweep, apps/relayer

No third-party code, no-referrer BUILT

The app loads nothing from third parties and sets a no-referrer policy. A content-security policy restricts scripts and styles to the app's own origin.

Does not hide: A compromised host or browser extension defeats everything. GitHub Pages cannot send security headers.

Source: apps/web index.html; MH-WEB-1 (done, with gaps)

Planned

Spec only. Nothing below is implemented.

Fresh payer wallet and sponsored payments PLANNED

Payer options (fresh wallet, permit relay, EIP-7702 sponsor, ERC-4337 paymaster) to cut the link to the payer main wallet.

Does not hide: Never hides the payer address itself, and a sponsor sees payer, amount and timing.

Source: privacy-specs/payer-hiding.md

Amount and timing blurring PLANNED

Round amounts, split a payment, use fixed denominations, multi-step delays, plus an honesty meter showing the crowd-size estimate.

Does not hide: Friction only; it does not make you anonymous and the spec says so.

Source: privacy-specs/amount-blurring.md

Sweep hygiene PLANNED

Fresh self-derived destination, no consolidation, address-free verification, buffer rounding, a known-wallet guard.

Does not hide: The sweep is still visible on-chain. It lowers self-inflicted leaks only.

Source: privacy-specs/hygiene.md

Delayed multi-hop forwarding (weak) PLANNED

Forward funds through several hops over time. Off by default, behind a blocking warning.

Does not hide: Followers and automated analysis link hops easily. Adds delay and cost, not unlinkability.

Source: privacy-specs/hops.md

Exit through an external pool PLANNED

Exit through Privacy Pools or Railgun, chosen by the user.

Does not hide: Depends on third-party contracts and their anonymity sets. Nothing has been run against any pool.

Source: privacy-specs/external-pools.md

Own shielded-pool exit PLANNED

A GhostPay-built pool with zero-knowledge proofs for the exit step.

Does not hide: Large, unbuilt, zero-knowledge circuits unaudited. Anonymity set would start at zero.

Source: privacy-specs/shielded-pool.md

Confidential token amounts PLANNED

Hide amounts with encrypted-amount tokens (for example Zama fhEVM, ERC-7984 draft) on supported testnets.

Does not hide: Relies on coprocessor or TEE trust. Payer and timing stay public.

Source: privacy-specs/confidential-tokens.md

Network-layer privacy PLANNED

Transport profiles for RPC and relayer calls: Tor, I2P outproxy, Nym, Oblivious HTTP, own node, quorum.

Does not hide: Hides your IP from the endpoint at best. Does not hide anything on-chain.

Source: privacy-specs/network-privacy.md

Private scanning PLANNED

Scan via an indexer over Tor, two-server PIR or a light client so your RPC learns less about which payments are yours.

Does not hide: Research-grade. Server-side trust assumptions apply.

Source: network-privacy.md; MH-SCAN-2/3

Metadata hardening menu PLANNED

About 30 independent switches (MH-*): minimal announcements, amount noise, gas bucketing, relayer batching, padding, multi-relayer, zero-log relayer, per-invoice meta-address, per-chain keys and more. A few (standard announcer, view tag, full-range scan) are already how the app works.

Does not hide: Each one hides one named leak and leaves others. Most are off by default and not built.

Source: privacy-specs/metadata-hardening.md

Bitcoin PayJoin PLANNED

Payee adds an input to the payment so common-input heuristics break (BIP-78, async BIP-77).

Does not hide: Not silent payments, not a mixer. Fallback broadcast stays possible. Testnet PoC only.

Source: privacy-specs/btc-payjoin.md

Experimental

Code exists but is not part of the app, or is unverified.

Bitcoin Silent Payments (BIP-352) EXPERIMENTAL

A library for keys, addresses, sender derivation, scanning and spending. Passes the official BIP-352 vectors.

Does not hide: Not wired into the web app or relayer; never run against a real network. Amounts and timing are still public.

Source: packages/btc-silent-payments

Policy accounts EXPERIMENTAL

Smart accounts that can only pay a fixed destination, after a delay, within limits. Contracts and a client library exist.

Does not hide: No app support, no recovery path, destination link becomes permanent. Do not use.

Source: packages/policy-scheme, PolicyAccount.sol

Tor Browser, your own setup EXPERIMENTAL

You can open any site in Tor Browser to hide your IP from the host and RPC.

Does not hide: Not an app feature. The app does not check it and we have not tested GhostPay with it.

Source: docs/remote/onion-i2p-research.md
