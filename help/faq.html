Help / FAQ

FAQ

What is hidden?

An invoice does not reveal which on-chain address is yours, because each payment goes to a fresh one-time address. The invoice details live after the # in the link, which is not sent to the web server. Your keys stay in your browser.

What is NOT hidden?

The payer address, amount, token, time and the fact that the GhostPay router was used are public on-chain. The sweep from the one-time address to your destination is public too. Your meta-address ties your invoices together.

Is it audited?

No. Only self-run and AI-assisted reviews exist, and the audit round has open findings. There is no independent third-party audit.

Can I use real money?

No. Do not. It is testnet only (Ethereum Sepolia and Base Sepolia), nothing is deployed, and contracts are immutable once deployed.

Is it live anywhere?

No. No contract is deployed. This site is a prototype on GitHub Pages, with payment disabled because there is no deployment manifest. It is not a hardened host: Pages cannot send security headers.

Is there an admin key?

The contracts are written with no owner, proxy, pause or admin function; the fee rate and recipient are fixed when a router is deployed. That is a design claim: it has not been independently audited. A consequence is that a bad fee recipient (for example one a token issuer blacklists) can only be fixed by deploying a new router.

What are the fees?

0.25 % when paying through the router. Nothing when paying the one-time address directly. A relayer sweep costs the larger of gas x 1.10 and 0.50 %, capped at 3 %. See Fees.

Can a payer skip the fee?

Yes, by paying directly. That is by design and stated in the app.

Why does sweeping reveal so much?

The payer knows the one-time address and can watch it. When you sweep, the chain shows payer to one-time address to your destination. Use a fresh destination and never your main wallet.

What does a relayer know?

The one-time address, destination, token, amount, fee, a hashed IP and timing. It can refuse or delay a request. It cannot change what you signed.

Who can read my invoice?

Anyone with the link. It is a bearer link. Browsers may keep it in history, though the app removes it from the address bar after reading.

What if I lose my keys?

Nobody can recover them, including the project. Back up the encrypted keystore and keep the passphrase separately.

Are the privacy options available?

Only a few. Most are planned specs. See Privacy options for exact status.

Does it support Bitcoin?

There is an experimental Silent Payments library. It is not in the app. See Bitcoin Silent Payments.

What licence is it under?

No licence has been chosen yet. That is an open decision for the owner.
