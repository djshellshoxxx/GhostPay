The whole flow

This is the path of one payment. Colour and label both tell you who acts: seller, payer, relayer, or the public chain.

In plain words

  • The link stays private to your browser. The invoice is stored after the #. Browsers do not send that part to the web server. Anyone who has the link can still read it, and your browser history may keep it.
  • Router or direct. Paying through the router costs 0.25 % and the router passes the rest on and posts the announcement. Paying the one-time address directly costs no GhostPay fee but skips the router’s checks. The app’s Pay page uses the router.
  • The announcement is public. It lets the seller find the payment, and it lets anyone list GhostPay payments.
  • Sweeping is where privacy is easiest to lose. The payer knows the one-time address and can see where you move the money. Use a fresh destination.

Nothing is deployed, so the app refuses to pay without a deployment manifest. The flow above is the design, tested with mocks.
