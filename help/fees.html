Fees

Nothing is deployed, so no fee is collected anywhere today. This is the designed schedule.

Fee table
FeeWhenAmount
CheckoutPay through the router0.25 % (25 bps) of gross, rounded down; none under 400 base units
Direct payPayer sends to the one-time addressNo GhostPay fee
Relayer sweepYou use a relayermax(gas cost x 1.10, 0.50 %), capped at 3 %, fixed in your signature
Policy account creationContracts only, no appFlat stablecoin fee (design value USD 3, owner to confirm)

Good to know

  • The fee only works where funds pass through GhostPay’s contract. A payer can bypass it, and a fork of the client can drop it. This is by design and disclosed.
  • The fee rate and recipient are fixed per deployment and shown in the app from the deployment manifest. The recipient is not in the source code.
  • Network gas, wallet fees and RPC charges are not GhostPay fees.
