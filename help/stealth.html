One-time addresses

A stealth address scheme (ERC-5564) lets a stranger make an address for you that only you can spend from, without talking to you first.

In plain words

  • You publish a meta-address: two public keys. They are not an address you receive to.
  • Each payer makes a one-off key, mixes it with your public viewing key and gets a secret only they and you can compute.
  • That secret shifts your spending public key into a brand-new address. Only your spending private key plus the same shift can spend it.
  • The payer posts the one-off public key in an announcement. You use your viewing key to spot payments meant for you.

What this does not hide

  • The one-time address, token, amount and time are public.
  • Your meta-address is a stable identifier. Two invoices from you can be tied together.
  • Whoever holds your viewing key can see all your incoming payments (not spend them).
