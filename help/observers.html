What observers see

GhostPay reduces address reuse. It does not make a payment private. Here is who learns what.

Who learns what
ObserverLearnsDoes not learn
Public chain analystPayer address, token, gross and net amounts, fee flow, router use, the announcement, timing, and the sweep source and destinationWhich seller owns a one-time address from chain data alone; invoice contents
Static host / CDNYour IP, user agent, files fetched, timingThe # part of the link, keys, invoice
RPC providerYour IP, blocks and addresses you query, transactions you sendViewing or spending keys; invoice
Relayer operatorHashed IP, one-time address, destination, token, amount, fee, timing; can censorKeys
Wallet providerTransaction requests, chain, connected accountsKeys of the one-time address
Anyone holding the invoice linkThe entire invoiceSeller keys
The payerThe one-time address they paid, so where you sweep itYour keys
Anyone with two of your invoicesBoth come from the same seller (same meta-address)Your private keys

Not hidden: payer, amount, token, time and the router call. A compromised website, browser extension or device defeats everything.
